The license covers **copyright**, not clinical validity. Reference facts must still be verified
against their primary sources (see [research/source-ledger.yaml](research/source-ledger.yaml))
before use. Clinical guideline organizations (ESMO, ESGO/ESTRO, NCCN, FIGO, ESPEN, etc.) own
their own materials; Oncoskill paraphrases and cites them and does not relicense their text.
